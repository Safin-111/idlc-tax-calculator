Wealth

Is your net wealth over Tk 4 crore, or do you own more than one car, or a home over 8,000 sq ft?
ⓘ What is wealth surcharge?
Wealth surcharge
An extra charge on top of your income tax, for people with a lot of wealth. Net wealth is everything you own minus what you owe.
APPLIES TO YOU IF
Net wealth over Tk 4 crore, or more than one car, or a home over 8,000 sq ft
EXAMPLE
Net wealth Tk 6 crore, income tax Tk 2,00,000. Surcharge is 10% of the tax: Tk 20,000.
